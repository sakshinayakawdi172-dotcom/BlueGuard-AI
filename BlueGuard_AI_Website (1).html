🌍 The Problem

Pollution can spread quickly, while monitoring is often slow, manual and expensive.

🧴

Plastic Pollution

Plastic and waste enter rivers, beaches and oceans, harming marine ecosystems.

⚠️

Late Detection

Pollution incidents and illegal dumping may be discovered only after damage has occurred.

🐢

Threat to Marine Life

Fish, turtles, coral and other organisms are affected by polluted water and habitats.

🤖 Our Smart Solution

BlueGuard AI creates an early-warning network for cleaner oceans.
